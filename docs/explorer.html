How decern establishes a caller

decern will not start until the deployment names how its callers are established. The five postures differ in how the caller is proven, and that also decides whom the caller may name and what the record says about it. From the kernel on, every request takes the same path: the solver-proven kernel, the recorded decision, the fail-closed serve. Choose a posture: the diagram, the traffic it carries and the steps follow your choice. The table at the end puts all five side by side.
