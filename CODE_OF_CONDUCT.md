# Code of Conduct

decern adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, in full:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming, and respectful community for everyone,
regardless of background or identity.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to a maintainer
via their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and enforcement follows the
Contributor Covenant v2.1 guidelines linked above.
